# ⛏️ SmartMine

## Digital Operations Intelligence for Small-Scale Mining

<p align="center">
  <strong>From extraction to production. From operational data to smarter decisions.</strong>
</p>

<p align="center">
  SmartMine is a digital operations platform designed to help small-scale mining operations
  record, monitor, understand and improve their daily activities — from material extraction
  and movement to crushing, grinding, processing, equipment management, fuel, maintenance,
  safety, inventory, costs and mineral production.
</p>

## 🌍 The Vision

Small-scale mining operations often depend on a combination of:

**Paper records + WhatsApp + Excel + manual calculations + experience**

SmartMine brings these operational activities together into one connected digital platform.

The goal is not simply to digitise paperwork.

> **SmartMine is designed to digitise the mining operation itself.**
