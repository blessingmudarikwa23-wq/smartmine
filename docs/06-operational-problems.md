# 06 — Operational Problems

## SmartMine
### Digital Operations Intelligence for Small-Scale Mining

**Document:** Operational Problems  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Foundation Document  
**Research Context:** Zimbabwe  
**Primary Sector:** Small-Scale Mining  

---

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies the operational problems that SmartMine is intended to address.

The problems are focused on the day-to-day realities of small-scale mining operations, particularly the challenges associated with recording, monitoring and connecting mining, processing and supporting activities.

SmartMine should solve practical operational problems rather than introduce technology for its own sake.

---

## 2. Fragmented Record Keeping

Operational information may be spread across:

- Paper notebooks
- Excel spreadsheets
- WhatsApp
- Receipts
- Separate registers
- Verbal communication
- Individual records

This creates fragmented information and makes it difficult to establish one reliable operational record.

SmartMine should provide a central location for operational information.

---

## 3. Manual Data Entry and Calculations

Many operational calculations may be performed manually.

Examples include:

- Production totals
- Fuel consumption
- Equipment hours
- Expenses
- Stock balances
- Material quantities
- Cost calculations

Manual calculations increase the possibility of:

- Errors
- Missing information
- Duplicate records
- Delayed reporting
- Inconsistent results

SmartMine should automate appropriate calculations while preserving the underlying operational records.

---

## 4. Poor Production Visibility

Mining operations need to understand what is being extracted, processed and produced.

Without structured records, it may be difficult to determine:

- Daily production
- Shift production
- Weekly production
- Material processed
- Production trends
- Production against targets

SmartMine should provide a clear production history and operational dashboard.

---

## 5. Limited Material Traceability

Material may move through several stages:

    Extraction
        ↓
    Movement
        ↓
    Crushing
        ↓
    Grinding
        ↓
    Processing
        ↓
    Recovery
        ↓
    Production

When these stages are recorded separately, the relationship between them can become unclear.

SmartMine should improve material traceability by connecting relevant operational records.

---

## 6. Crushing and Grinding Visibility

Crushers and grinding mills can represent important processing stages.

However, operations may lack structured information about:

- Operating hours
- Material processed
- Output
- Downtime
- Downtime reasons
- Operators
- Fuel or energy consumption

This makes it difficult to measure processing performance.

SmartMine should provide dedicated workflows for crushing and grinding.

---

## 7. Equipment Downtime

Equipment downtime can interrupt production and increase operational costs.

Potential challenges include:

- Unexpected equipment failure
- Poor downtime recording
- Limited downtime history
- Difficulty identifying recurring failures
- Limited equipment performance visibility

SmartMine should record:

- Equipment
- Downtime duration
- Date
- Shift
- Reason
- Operational impact
- Resolution

This can provide a foundation for future equipment-performance analysis.

---

## 8. Reactive Maintenance

Maintenance may sometimes occur after equipment has already failed.

This can create:

    Equipment Failure
          ↓
       Downtime
          ↓
    Repair Required
          ↓
    Production Disrupted

Without reliable operating-hour and maintenance records, planned maintenance becomes more difficult.

SmartMine should connect:

    Equipment
       ↓
    Operating Hours
       ↓
    Maintenance Schedule
       ↓
    Maintenance Activity
       ↓
    Maintenance History

---

## 9. Fuel Management Problems

Fuel consumption can be difficult to monitor when fuel records are incomplete or disconnected from equipment usage.

Problems may include:

- Unclear fuel balances
- Limited equipment-level consumption
- Manual calculations
- Missing transactions
- Difficulty identifying unusual consumption

SmartMine should provide:

- Fuel receipts
- Fuel stock
- Fuel issues
- Equipment allocation
- Consumption records
- Historical analysis

---

## 10. Inventory and Spare Parts Problems

Mining operations depend on supplies and spare parts.

Poor inventory visibility can lead to:

- Stock shortages
- Delayed repairs
- Unplanned purchases
- Excess stock
- Poor tracking of issued items

SmartMine should help users monitor:

    Stock Received
        ↓
    Stock Available
        ↓
    Stock Issued
        ↓
    Stock Used
        ↓
    Remaining Stock

Minimum-stock alerts can help identify items requiring attention.

---

## 11. Workforce and Shift Coordination

Operational activities depend on workers being assigned to the correct areas, shifts and equipment.

Challenges may include:

- Unclear shift assignments
- Incomplete attendance records
- Limited activity tracking
- Poor connection between workers and equipment

SmartMine should connect:

    Worker
       ↓
    Shift
       ↓
    Work Area
       ↓
    Equipment
       ↓
    Activity

The system should remain lightweight and focused on operational needs.

---

## 12. Safety Record Management

Safety information needs to be captured consistently.

Operational challenges may include:

- Incomplete inspections
- Poor hazard tracking
- Lost incident information
- Unresolved corrective actions
- Limited historical safety visibility

SmartMine should provide structured records for:

- Inspections
- Hazards
- Incidents
- Near misses
- Observations
- Corrective actions

The platform should support safety management but not replace professional safety systems or legal requirements.

---

## 13. Limited Cost Visibility

Mining operations need to understand operational costs.

Without connected records, it may be difficult to determine:

- Total operating costs
- Fuel costs
- Maintenance costs
- Processing costs
- Transport costs
- Supply costs
- Cost trends

SmartMine should connect expense information with operational activities where appropriate.

This can eventually support indicators such as:

    Operating Cost
          ÷
    Material Processed
          =
    Cost per Tonne

---

## 14. Limited Operational Intelligence

When operational data is fragmented, managers may struggle to identify trends.

For example, they may not easily see:

- Increasing equipment downtime
- Increasing fuel consumption
- Declining processing throughput
- Rising maintenance costs
- Inventory shortages
- Production changes

SmartMine should transform operational records into:

    DATA
      ↓
    DASHBOARDS
      ↓
    ANALYTICS
      ↓
    ALERTS
      ↓
    INSIGHTS

Advanced AI should come after reliable operational data has been established.

---

## 15. The Core Operational Challenge

The individual problems identified in this document are connected.

They can be represented as:

    FRAGMENTED RECORDS
            ↓
    LIMITED VISIBILITY
            ↓
    MANUAL CALCULATIONS
            ↓
    DELAYED INFORMATION
            ↓
    DIFFICULT DECISION-MAKING
            ↓
    OPERATIONAL INEFFICIENCIES

SmartMine aims to reverse this pattern:

    DAILY ACTIVITIES
          ↓
    STRUCTURED RECORDS
          ↓
    CONNECTED DATA
          ↓
    REAL-TIME / TIMELY VISIBILITY
          ↓
    ANALYTICS
          ↓
    OPERATIONAL INSIGHTS
          ↓
    BETTER DECISIONS
          ↓
    CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

The fundamental operational problem SmartMine seeks to solve is:

> **Small-scale mining operations need a practical way to capture, connect and understand the information generated by their daily activities.**

SmartMine should therefore focus on solving real operational problems across the mining workflow rather than becoming a collection of unrelated software features.

This document provides the problem foundation for the next stage:

**07 — Solution Concept**

which will define how SmartMine will respond to these operational problems.
