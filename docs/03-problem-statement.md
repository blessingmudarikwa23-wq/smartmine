# SmartMine — Problem Statement

## 1. Overview

Small-scale mining operations involve multiple interconnected activities that must work together for the operation to remain productive and financially sustainable.

These activities may include:

- Mineral extraction.
- Material transportation.
- Stone and ore crushing.
- Grinding.
- Mineral processing.
- Mineral recovery.
- Equipment operation.
- Equipment maintenance.
- Fuel management.
- Workforce activities.
- Inventory and consumables.
- Safety activities.
- Operational expenses.
- Production recording.
- Operational reporting.

Despite the importance of these activities, many small-scale mining operations may rely on fragmented and predominantly manual methods for recording and managing operational information.

This can make it difficult for owners, managers, supervisors, and operational personnel to obtain a reliable and timely understanding of what is happening across the mining operation.

SmartMine is intended to address this operational information gap through a centralized digital operations platform.

---

## 2. Core Problem

The central problem SmartMine aims to address is:

> **Small-scale mining operations can lack a centralized, structured, and practical system for recording, monitoring, connecting, and analyzing their daily operational activities.**

When information is distributed across notebooks, spreadsheets, receipts, messages, paper forms, and verbal communication, important operational information can become difficult to access, compare, verify, and analyze.

This can reduce operational visibility and make decision-making more reactive.
