# 03 — Problem Statement

## SmartMine
### Digital Operations Intelligence for Small-Scale Mining

**Document:** Problem Statement  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Foundation Document  
**Research Context:** Zimbabwe  
**Target Sector:** Small-Scale Mining  

---

## 1. Problem Overview

Small-scale mining plays an important role in Zimbabwe's mining economy and provides livelihoods and economic activity across many mining communities.

However, many small-scale mining operations still depend heavily on fragmented, manual and informal methods for managing daily operations.

Operational information may be recorded using:

- Paper notebooks
- WhatsApp messages
- Excel spreadsheets
- Manual calculations
- Verbal communication
- Separate records
- Individual memory

This makes it difficult for mine owners and supervisors to obtain a reliable, connected view of what is happening across the operation.

SmartMine addresses this problem by creating a digital operational platform that connects mining activities, processing, equipment, resources, costs, safety and production information.

---

## 2. The Core Problem

The central problem is not simply a lack of software.

The deeper problem is:

> **Small-scale mining operations often lack a simple, connected and practical system for capturing, organising and understanding their operational data.**

Information becomes fragmented across different people, locations and tools.

For example:

    Production Record
          +
    Fuel Record
          +
    Equipment Record
          +
    Maintenance Record
          +
    Expense Record
          +
    Worker Record

may all exist separately.

This makes it difficult to understand how one operational activity affects another.

SmartMine aims to connect these records into one operational view.

---

## 3. Fragmented Operational Information

A typical operation may use different methods for different activities.

For example:

    Paper Notebook
        ↓
    Daily Production

    WhatsApp
        ↓
    Equipment Updates

    Excel
        ↓
    Expenses

    Memory
        ↓
    Maintenance Information

    Verbal Communication
        ↓
    Shift Information

The result is fragmented information.

Problems may include:

- Duplicate records
- Missing information
- Delayed updates
- Inconsistent figures
- Difficult reporting
- Limited historical visibility
- Manual calculations
- Poor traceability

SmartMine should provide a central operational record.

---

## 4. Limited Visibility Across the Mining Workflow

Mining operations involve connected activities.

The material may move through:

    Extraction
        ↓
    Material Movement
        ↓
    Crushing
        ↓
    Grinding
        ↓
    Processing
        ↓
    Recovery
        ↓
    Production
        ↓
    Sales

However, operational records may not follow the material through the complete workflow.

This makes it difficult to answer questions such as:

- How much material was extracted?
- How much reached the crusher?
- How much was processed?
- How much was produced?
- Where did delays occur?
- Where did material volumes change?
- Which stage created the greatest bottleneck?

SmartMine aims to create greater visibility across the operational chain.

---

## 5. Crushing and Grinding Challenges

Crushing and grinding are important stages in many mining and mineral-processing operations.

Yet information about these activities may be recorded inconsistently.

Important information can include:

- Crusher or mill used
- Operator
- Shift
- Material input
- Material output
- Operating hours
- Downtime
- Downtime reason
- Fuel or energy usage
- Production

Without structured records, it becomes difficult to determine:

- Which equipment is performing best
- How much material is being processed
- How much downtime is occurring
- Whether throughput is improving
- Whether maintenance is affecting production

SmartMine should make crushing and grinding measurable operational activities.

---

## 6. Equipment Downtime and Maintenance

Equipment failure and downtime can directly affect production.

Small-scale mining operations may have limited visibility into:

- Equipment operating hours
- Maintenance schedules
- Downtime
- Repairs
- Parts used
- Maintenance costs
- Equipment availability

Maintenance may sometimes be reactive rather than planned.

The operational challenge is:

    Equipment Usage
          ↓
    Operating Hours
          ↓
    Maintenance Requirement
          ↓
    Maintenance Action

Without reliable records, it becomes difficult to anticipate maintenance requirements or understand the operational cost of equipment downtime.

SmartMine should provide structured equipment and maintenance records that support better operational planning.

---

## 7. Fuel and Resource Management

Fuel and other operational resources represent important costs for many mining operations.

Without structured tracking, it may be difficult to determine:

- How much fuel was received
- How much fuel was issued
- Which equipment used it
- How much fuel remains
- How consumption changes over time
- Whether consumption is consistent with operational activity

The basic operational challenge is:

    Fuel Received
          ↓
    Fuel Stock
          ↓
    Fuel Issued
          ↓
    Equipment
          ↓
    Consumption

SmartMine should connect fuel information with equipment and operational activity.

---

## 8. Production and Material Measurement

Production information is one of the most important forms of operational data.

However, production figures can become difficult to manage when measurements are recorded manually or inconsistently.

Potential challenges include:

- Different units
- Missing dates
- Incomplete shift records
- Inconsistent measurements
- Difficult historical comparisons
- Manual calculations
- Limited production trends

SmartMine should provide structured production records.

Where measurements are available, the platform should help users understand:

- Material extracted
- Material processed
- Production output
- Throughput
- Production targets
- Historical trends

SmartMine should not invent measurements that the operation cannot reliably obtain.

---

## 9. Workforce and Shift Visibility

Mining operations depend on workers performing activities across different areas.

However, workforce information may be disconnected from operational activities.

It can become difficult to determine:

- Who worked
- Which shift they worked
- Where they worked
- Which equipment they operated
- Which activity they performed

The operational relationship should be:

    Worker
       ↓
    Shift
       ↓
    Work Area
       ↓
    Equipment
       ↓
    Activity

SmartMine should provide lightweight workforce and shift visibility without attempting to become a complete enterprise HR system.

---

## 10. Safety and Incident Tracking

Mining operations require strong attention to workplace safety.

Important safety information may include:

- Safety inspections
- PPE checks
- Equipment inspections
- Hazards
- Incidents
- Near misses
- Safety observations
- Corrective actions

When these records are fragmented or paper-based, it may become difficult to track whether issues were reported, assigned and resolved.

SmartMine should provide a structured safety record and corrective-action workflow.

The platform should support operational visibility but should not replace professional safety management, qualified safety personnel or applicable regulatory requirements.

---

## 11. Inventory and Spare Parts Challenges

Mining operations require consumables, tools and spare parts to keep equipment running.

Examples include:

- Belts
- Bearings
- Lubricants
- Tools
- PPE
- Maintenance materials
- Processing consumables
- Other spare parts

Poor inventory visibility can create operational problems.

For example:

    Equipment Requires Part
             ↓
    Part Not Available
             ↓
       Repair Delayed
             ↓
        Downtime
             ↓
      Production Impact

SmartMine should help track:

- Stock levels
- Stock received
- Stock issued
- Stock used
- Remaining stock
- Minimum stock levels

This creates a connection between inventory and operational continuity.

---

## 12. Cost and Financial Visibility

Small-scale mining operations need to understand where operational money is being spent.

However, expenses may be recorded across different notebooks, spreadsheets or informal records.

Potential expense categories include:

- Fuel
- Maintenance
- Labour
- Transport
- Equipment
- Supplies
- Processing
- Safety
- Other operational costs

Without structured information, it becomes difficult to answer:

- What are our biggest costs?
- How much did the operation spend this week?
- How much did we spend on fuel?
- How much did maintenance cost?
- What is the approximate cost per tonne?

SmartMine should connect operational expenses with production data where reliable information exists.

---

## 13. The Zimbabwean Small-Scale Mining Context

Zimbabwe has a significant small-scale and artisanal mining sector, with gold being particularly important alongside other minerals.

The SmartMine product must therefore be informed by the realities of Zimbabwean small-scale mining rather than assumptions based on large multinational mining companies.

Research should consider:

- Small-scale and artisanal mining practices
- Gold production and recovery activities
- Other mineral operations
- Common equipment
- Crushing and grinding practices
- Processing workflows
- Workforce structures
- Operational costs
- Safety challenges
- Access to technology
- Connectivity
- Record-keeping practices
- Relevant Zimbabwean mining regulations and institutional requirements

SmartMine should recognise that mining operations differ significantly depending on mineral, location, equipment, scale and processing method.

The platform should therefore support configurable workflows rather than imposing one fixed mining model.

SmartMine is intended to address the following operational problems:

1. Fragmented operational records
2. Poor visibility across the mining workflow
3. Inconsistent production records
4. Limited crushing and grinding visibility
5. Equipment downtime visibility challenges
6. Reactive maintenance tracking
7. Poor fuel visibility
8. Limited workforce and shift visibility
9. Fragmented safety records
10. Poor inventory visibility
11. Limited expense visibility
12. Difficult operational reporting
13. Manual calculations
14. Limited historical analysis
15. Lack of connected operational intelligence

SmartMine will address these problems through a connected operational platform.

The intended transformation is:

    FRAGMENTED RECORDS
           ↓
    STRUCTURED DATA
           ↓
    CONNECTED OPERATIONS
           ↓
    OPERATIONAL VISIBILITY
           ↓
    ANALYTICS
           ↓
    BETTER DECISIONS

The problem SmartMine addresses can ultimately be expressed as:

> **Small-scale mining operations need a practical way to turn fragmented daily operational information into a connected understanding of their mine.**

SmartMine's response is:

    RECORD
       ↓
    CONNECT
       ↓
    MONITOR
       ↓
    ANALYSE
       ↓
    IMPROVE

The platform should help users move from:

    Paper
    +
    WhatsApp
    +
    Excel
    +
    Manual Calculations
    +
    Memory

toward:

    One Connected Operational Platform
               ↓
        Reliable Records
               ↓
        Operational Visibility
               ↓
        Better Decisions

The ultimate problem-solving objective is:

> **Give small-scale mining operations the operational visibility they need to understand what is happening, identify problems earlier and make better evidence-based decisions.**
