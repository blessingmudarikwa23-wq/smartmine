# 07 — Solution Concept

## SmartMine
### Digital Operations Intelligence for Small-Scale Mining

**Document:** Solution Concept  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Foundation Document  
**Research Context:** Zimbabwe  
**Primary Sector:** Small-Scale Mining  

---

## 1. Solution Overview

SmartMine is proposed as a connected digital operations platform for small-scale mining operations.

The platform will bring mining, processing and supporting activities into one operational environment.

The solution will connect:

- Extraction
- Material movement
- Crushing
- Grinding
- Processing
- Recovery
- Production
- Equipment
- Maintenance
- Fuel
- Workforce
- Inventory
- Safety
- Expenses
- Sales

The objective is to provide a clear digital view of the operation.

---

## 2. From Fragmented Records to One Platform

The current problem can be represented as:

    PAPER
       +
    WHATSAPP
       +
    EXCEL
       +
    MANUAL CALCULATIONS
       +
    VERBAL REPORTS
       ↓
    FRAGMENTED INFORMATION

SmartMine changes this to:

    DAILY OPERATIONS
          ↓
    SMARTMINE
          ↓
    CONNECTED DATA
          ↓
    OPERATIONAL VISIBILITY

The platform becomes a central operational record for the mine.

---

## 3. Digital Mine Concept

SmartMine will create a Digital Mine that represents the operational structure of a mining site.

The Digital Mine will connect:

    Mine
     ├── Areas
     ├── Materials
     ├── Production
     ├── Processing
     ├── Equipment
     ├── Workers
     ├── Fuel
     ├── Maintenance
     ├── Inventory
     ├── Safety
     ├── Expenses
     └── Sales

Each area should contribute information to the wider operational picture.

---

## 4. Connected Mining Workflow

SmartMine will connect the primary material workflow:

    EXTRACTION
         ↓
    MATERIAL MOVEMENT
         ↓
    CRUSHING
         ↓
    GRINDING
         ↓
    PROCESSING
         ↓
    RECOVERY
         ↓
    PRODUCTION
         ↓
    SALES

Supporting information can then be connected to the relevant stage.

For example:

    Production
        ↕
    Equipment
        ↕
    Fuel
        ↕
    Maintenance
        ↕
    Expenses

This creates operational relationships that are difficult to see in disconnected records.

---

## 5. Operational Data Capture

SmartMine should make recording daily activities simple.

Users should be able to record:

- Production
- Material movement
- Equipment usage
- Downtime
- Fuel transactions
- Maintenance
- Inventory transactions
- Safety activities
- Expenses
- Shifts

The system should avoid unnecessary data-entry requirements.

The objective is:

> **Capture the information that matters without slowing down the operation.**

---

## 6. Crushing and Grinding Solution

SmartMine will provide dedicated workflows for crushing and grinding.

A crushing workflow can record:

- Crusher
- Operator
- Shift
- Material
- Input
- Output
- Operating hours
- Downtime
- Downtime reason
- Fuel or energy
- Notes

A grinding workflow can record:

- Grinding mill
- Operator
- Shift
- Material
- Quantity processed
- Operating hours
- Downtime
- Fuel or energy
- Notes

These records will contribute to production and equipment analysis.

---

## 7. Equipment Intelligence

SmartMine will turn equipment records into operational information.

For each equipment asset, the platform can maintain:

- Equipment identity
- Status
- Location
- Operator
- Operating hours
- Usage
- Downtime
- Maintenance
- Repairs
- Fuel consumption

The long-term objective is:

    EQUIPMENT
        ↓
    USAGE
        ↓
    PERFORMANCE
        ↓
    MAINTENANCE
        ↓
    RELIABILITY

This can help managers understand equipment performance over time.

---

## 8. Maintenance Management

SmartMine will support planned and corrective maintenance.

The workflow is:

    Equipment
        ↓
    Operating Hours
        ↓
    Maintenance Requirement
        ↓
    Maintenance Record
        ↓
    Parts Used
        ↓
    Cost
        ↓
    Downtime
        ↓
    Completion

Maintenance history will provide a foundation for future reliability analytics.

The system should help users move gradually from reactive maintenance toward more structured maintenance planning.

Inventory:

    RECEIVED

    STOCK

## 10. Workforce and Safety Solution

SmartMine will connect workers with operational activities.

    WORKER
       ↓
    SHIFT
       ↓
    WORK AREA
       ↓
    EQUIPMENT
       ↓
    ACTIVITY

The safety component will provide structured records for:

- Inspections
- Hazards
- Incidents
- Near misses
- PPE checks
- Safety observations
- Corrective actions

The objective is improved visibility rather than replacing specialist safety systems.

---

## 11. Production and Cost Intelligence

SmartMine will connect production and financial information.

Production:

    MATERIAL
       ↓
    PROCESSING
       ↓
    OUTPUT
       ↓
    PRODUCTION

Costs:

    FUEL
    MAINTENANCE
    LABOUR
    TRANSPORT
    SUPPLIES
    PROCESSING
       ↓
    OPERATING COST

Where reliable data exists, SmartMine can eventually calculate:

    Operating Cost
          ÷
    Material Processed
          =
    Cost per Tonne

This provides more useful information than isolated expense totals.

---

## 12. Operational Dashboard

SmartMine will provide dashboards that summarise the state of the operation.

Potential dashboard indicators include:

- Material extracted
- Material processed
- Production
- Equipment availability
- Downtime
- Fuel usage
- Maintenance
- Inventory
- Expenses
- Safety activity

The dashboard should answer:

> **What is happening across the mine right now or during the selected period?**

Users should be able to move from high-level information into the underlying operational records.

---

## 13. Alerts and Intelligence

SmartMine should gradually introduce operational alerts.

Examples include:

    ⚠ Equipment maintenance due

    ⚠ Diesel stock approaching minimum

    ⚠ Crusher downtime increasing

    ⚠ Inventory below minimum level

    ⚠ Production below target

These alerts should be based on recorded operational data.

The intelligence pathway is:

    DATA
      ↓
    RULES
      ↓
    ALERTS
      ↓
    ANALYTICS
      ↓
    INSIGHTS
      ↓
    AI

AI should not be introduced simply because it is technically possible.

---

## 14. Configurable and Practical Design

SmartMine should not assume that every Zimbabwean mining operation is identical.

The platform should allow configuration based on:

- Mine type
- Mineral
- Equipment
- Processing workflow
- Measurement units
- Operational roles
- Site structure

The product should also be:

- Mobile-friendly
- Simple to use
- Clear
- Fast
- Suitable for field environments
- Designed with future offline capability in mind

The solution should adapt to the operation rather than forcing every operation into one rigid workflow.

---

## 15. The SmartMine Solution

The complete SmartMine solution can be represented as:

    PEOPLE
       ↓
    DAILY OPERATIONS
       ↓
    SMARTMINE
       ↓
    CONNECTED OPERATIONAL DATA
       ↓
    DASHBOARDS
       ↓
    ALERTS
       ↓
    ANALYTICS
       ↓
    OPERATIONAL INTELLIGENCE
       ↓
    BETTER DECISIONS
       ↓
    CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

The solution is built around three fundamental capabilities:

### OPERATE

Record and manage daily mining activities.

### MONITOR

Understand production, equipment, fuel, maintenance, safety, inventory and costs.

### IMPROVE

Use operational data to identify problems, trends, opportunities and potential improvements.

The ultimate solution statement is:

> **SmartMine provides small-scale mining operations with a connected digital environment for recording, monitoring and improving the activities that keep the mine operating.**

SmartMine is therefore not simply a mining management application.

It is the foundation for:

> **Digital Operations Intelligence for Small-Scale Mining.**

**08 — Core Features**

which will define the specific product capabilities required to deliver the SmartMine vision.
