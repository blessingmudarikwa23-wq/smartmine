# Target Users

## 1. Overview

SmartChain Nexus is designed as an AI-powered enterprise supply chain management platform that supports organizations in managing, monitoring, analysing, and improving their supply chain operations.

The platform is intended for users who are responsible for supply chain planning, procurement, inventory, suppliers, warehousing, logistics, sales, business intelligence, data analysis, and operational decision-making.

SmartChain Nexus is designed to provide different users with access to the information, workflows, analytics, and decision-support capabilities relevant to their responsibilities.

The platform should remain practical and intuitive while providing sufficient functionality for organizations managing increasingly complex supply chain operations.

# 16. User Needs Across the Platform

Although different users have different responsibilities, SmartChain Nexus is built around several common needs.

### Visibility

Users need a clear view of the information relevant to their responsibilities.

### Accuracy

Users need reliable and consistent operational information.

### Efficiency

Users need to reduce unnecessary manual processes and repetitive work.

### Monitoring

Users need to monitor performance, inventory, suppliers, procurement, logistics, and operational activities.

### Analysis

Users need to understand what is happening within the business and why it is happening.

### Prediction

Users need to anticipate future demand, sales, risks, and operational changes.

### Decision Support

Users need actionable information that helps them make better operational and strategic decisions.

### Collaboration

Different departments need to work from connected information rather than isolated records.

# 17. User Experience Principles

SmartChain Nexus should be designed around the following user experience principles:

## 17.1 Simplicity

Users should be able to understand the purpose of each feature without unnecessary complexity.

## 17.2 Role Relevance

Users should primarily see information and functionality relevant to their responsibilities.

## 17.3 Actionable Information

Dashboards and analytics should help users understand what action may be required.

## 17.4 Consistency

Navigation, terminology, forms, dashboards, tables, and reporting interfaces should remain consistent throughout the platform.

## 17.5 Data Visibility

Important operational information should be easy to locate and understand.

## 17.6 Progressive Complexity

Basic users should be able to perform routine tasks without needing advanced technical knowledge, while advanced users should have access to analytics and decision-support capabilities.

# 18. User Role and Access Model

SmartChain Nexus should use role-based access to ensure that users have appropriate access to platform functionality.

A preliminary access model includes:

| User Role | Primary Access |
|---|---|
| Administrator | Full system configuration and management |
| Executive | Executive dashboards, reports, KPIs and strategic insights |
| Supply Chain Manager | Supply chain monitoring, analytics and planning |
| Procurement Manager | Procurement, purchase orders and supplier management |
| Inventory Manager | Inventory monitoring and optimization |
| Warehouse Manager | Warehouse and inventory operations |
| Logistics Manager | Logistics and transportation operations |
| Supplier Manager | Supplier and vendor performance |
| Sales Manager | Sales and revenue analysis |
| Operations Manager | Operational performance and analytics |
| BI Analyst | Business intelligence and reporting |
| Data Analyst | Data analysis and analytics |
| Data Scientist | Predictive analytics and data science |
| Finance/Admin | Financial and administrative information |

This access model may be refined as authentication, authorization, and workflow requirements are further developed.
